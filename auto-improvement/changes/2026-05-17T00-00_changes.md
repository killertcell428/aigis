### Implemented

**New `comp_biometric_sensitive_categ` pattern** added to `COMPLIANCE_TRANSPARENCY_PATTERNS` in `aigis/filters/patterns.py`.

Catches requests to build AI systems that use biometric data to infer or deduce:
- Race / ethnicity
- Political opinions
- Religious or philosophical beliefs
- Sexual orientation
- Trade union membership

Biometric modalities covered: facial recognition, voice analysis, gait analysis, fingerprints, iris scans, retina scans.

Score: 70 (same as `comp_social_scoring_request`; highest-penalty tier).
Category: `compliance_violation`.

### What changed for users

- Users who send prompts asking aigis-protected agents to build biometric attribute inference systems will now see a `compliance_violation` flag with an EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(g) citation, blocking instructions that violate the prohibition before the agent acts on them.
- The prohibition has been enforceable since 2 February 2025; this is a coverage gap that now gets closed.

### What was only documented or deferred

- China GB/T 45654-2025 compliance template — deferred; English-language technical analysis too sparse.
- NIST IR 8596 mapping update — deferred until initial public draft published (summer 2026).
