## Summary

**Researched:** Fourth pass of the multi-agent domain. New material covered since the prior pass
(2026-05-15):
- **AgentLAB benchmark** (arxiv:2602.16901, Feb 2026) — task injection identified as #1
  Hijacking-class attack (31% of all successful attacks, up to 93.1% ASR on Qwen-3, 79.9% on
  GPT-4o) across 28 environments and 644 security test cases. Objective drifting and task injection
  are the two most prevalent real-world multi-agent attack patterns.
- **Tool-call chain resource amplification DoS** (arxiv:2601.10955, Jan 2026) — calibration-loop
  attack embeds `[SEGMENT t=N]` protocol in malicious tool responses, achieving up to 658x token
  amplification and 100–561x energy consumption. 96–97% attack success rate across 6 tested
  models. The specific syntactic signature (`[SEGMENT t=N]` + "call again") enables low-FP-risk
  detection.
- **Indirect goal manipulation** via collaborative task refinement (OWASP Agentic Top 10 2026) —
  67% success rate on production deployments; "forget original goal" imperative language is a
  high-confidence signal.
- **False orchestrator authority + goal override compound attack** (Microsoft Security Research
  2026) — combining false authority attribution with goal redefinition achieves higher ASR than
  either element alone.

**Implemented:** Two new pattern groups added to `aigis/multi_agent/message_scanner.py`:

1. **`_GOAL_HIJACKING_PATTERNS`** (5 patterns: 3 EN + 2 JA) — covers task injection and
   objective drifting:
   - `ma_goal_header_en`: explicit override header framing ("new task:", "updated objective:",
     "your new goal:"). Score 35. Category: `injection_relay`.
   - `ma_goal_authority_en`: false attribution of goal change to orchestrator/system/coordinator
     ("the orchestrator has updated your task to..."). Score 35. Category: `injection_relay`.
   - `ma_goal_abandon_en`: imperative goal abandonment ("forget/discard/abandon your original
     task/goal"). Score 35. Category: `injection_relay`.
   - `ma_goal_header_ja`: Japanese equivalents for explicit override header framing.
     Score 35. Category: `injection_relay`.
   - `ma_goal_abandon_ja`: Japanese equivalents for goal abandonment imperative.
     Score 35. Category: `injection_relay`.

2. **`_RESOURCE_BOMB_PATTERNS`** (1 EN pattern) — covers tool-call chain resource amplification
   DoS:
   - `ma_resource_bomb`: combined regex detecting `[SEGMENT t=N]` calibration-loop marker
     combined with "call again" / "re-call" / "provide complete sequence" directives in tool
     responses. Score 35. Category: `injection_relay`.
