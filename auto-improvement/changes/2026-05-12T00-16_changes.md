## Summary

Researched recent developments in the EU AI Act GPAI chapter, NIST CAISI initiative, CSA Agentic AI profile, and ISO/IEC 42001. Implemented one candidate hardening: a new policy template for General-Purpose AI (GPAI) model providers under EU AI Act Art. 53-55. Three candidates were deferred.

## What was implemented

**New file:** `policy_templates/gpai_provider.yaml`

A policy template for organizations that develop or distribute General-Purpose AI models subject to EU AI Act Chapter V (Art. 53-55). The template provides five custom detection rules:

| Rule ID | Article | What it detects | Score delta |
|---------|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| `gpai_model_eval_bypass` | Art. 55 | Instructions to skip, bypass, falsify, or omit model evaluation or adversarial testing | 65 |
| `gpai_systemic_risk_concealment` | Art. 55 | Instructions to hide, underreport, or misrepresent compute scale (FLOPs) to avoid the 10²⁵-FLOP notification duty | 70 |
| `gpai_training_data_bypass` | Art. 53 | Instructions to hide, omit, or skip training data documentation or the mandatory EU training data summary template | 60 |
| `gpai_incident_suppression` | Art. 55 | Instructions to not report incidents to the AI Office, conceal safety failures from regulators, or suppress incident reports | 65 |
| `gpai_copyright_circumvention` | Art. 53 | Instructions to ignore robots.txt, bypass TDM opt-out signals, or scrape training data without respecting copyright restrictions | 50 |

**New file:** `tests/test_gpai_provider.py`

19 tests across 6 test classes covering all five custom rules plus benign passthrough cases. Tests are skipped automatically when PyYAML is not installed (consistent with pre-existing behavior for other YAML policy tests).

## What changed for users

GPAI model providers deploying aigis can now use `policy_templates/gpai_provider.yaml` as their base policy. The template catches prompt-level attempts to bypass the most critical EU AI Act compliance obligations before they reach the model.

## Implementation caveats

- The custom rules use multi-line YAML string values with implicit concatenation. Patterns should be verified against real-world inputs when PyYAML is installed in a test environment.
- The `gpai_copyright_circumvention` rule has a lower score (50) than the others because isolated copyright discussion phrases could appear in legitimate compliance planning; the pattern is anchored to bypass/ignore intent verbs.
