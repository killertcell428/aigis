## Summary

**What was researched:** Fresh coverage of adversarial creative-format jailbreaks
(`jb_poetry_harmful_framing`, pending since pass 2), single-message payload splitting
(`jb_payload_splitting`, pending since pass 2), and newly published work on multilingual
bypass, activation-space suppression (COSMIC), embodied AI jailbreaking, and
representation-level evasion. The latter four are not detectable by rule-based regex.

**What was implemented:** Two new `DetectionPattern` entries added to
`JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS` in `aigis/filters/patterns.py`:
1. `jb_poetry_harmful_framing` — detects poem/rap/ballad/song directive + harmful topic
   keyword within 200 characters (score 55).
2. `jb_payload_splitting` — detects three-or-more numbered steps with a dangerous keyword
   appearing in step 3 or later (score 45).

**What changed for users:** Two new input-side jailbreak detections are active by default.
Agents processing user queries will now flag creative-format wrapping (poetry jailbreaks)
and numbered step decomposition attacks that disguise harmful intent.

**What was only documented or deferred:** Multilingual bypass, COSMIC activation
suppression, and embodied AI jailbreak research are documented in the research file but
not implementable as rule-based patterns.

## Implementation Caveats

- `jb_payload_splitting` uses a score of 45 (MEDIUM) because numbered step lists are
  common in legitimate multi-part questions (tutorials, recipes, troubleshooting). The
  rule requires 3+ numbered steps AND a dangerous keyword in step 3+, which limits false
  positives while covering the attack pattern.
- `jb_poetry_harmful_framing` uses `[\s\S]{0,200}?` (non-greedy, bounded) to avoid
  ReDoS risk, consistent with other patterns in the module.
