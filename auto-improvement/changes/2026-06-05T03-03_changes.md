### Implemented
Added `_TASK_INJECTION_PATTERNS` (3 rules) to `aigis/multi_agent/message_scanner.py`:
1. Fabricated bracketed policy/override codes: `[POLICY_MISMATCH]`, `[COMPLIANCE_REQUIRED]`,
   `[SECURITY_OVERRIDE]`, `[ADMIN_DIRECTIVE]`, `[OVERRIDE_ACTIVE]`, `[BYPASS_MODE]`,
   `[POLICY_VIOLATION]` — the AgentLAB three-phase attack syntax.
2. Explicit goal-replacement phrasing: "your new/updated task/goal/objective is:".
3. Task-abandonment instructions: "ignore/forget/abandon your original/previous
   task/goal/objective/instructions".

### What changed for users
`AgentMessageScanner` now detects a class of mid-workflow task-hijacking attacks that were
not covered by earlier patterns. Specifically: an adversary who embeds fabricated compliance
error codes or explicit task-replacement commands in inter-agent messages or tool results
can no longer redirect the receiving agent's goal without being flagged.

## Implementation caveats
The three new patterns are additive and do not alter existing scoring behavior. They use
the same `injection_relay` category and 35-point scoring delta as all other patterns, capped
at 60 per category. False-positive risk is low: the bracketed codes (`[POLICY_MISMATCH]`,
etc.) are highly specific and unlikely to appear in legitimate tool results. The goal-
replacement and task-abandonment phrases may occasionally match edge cases in legitimate
workflow orchestration messages (e.g., "your new task is: summarize the results"), but in
those cases the risk score delta of 35 would typically stay below the block threshold (60).
