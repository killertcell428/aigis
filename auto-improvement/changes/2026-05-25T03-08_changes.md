## Summary

### What was researched
Seven jailbreak techniques published between April 2025 and May 2026:
1. Humor-framed fixed-template attack (arxiv:2504.06577, ACL LLMSEC 2025) — Psst/hahaha/*whispers*/veracity template
2. Adversarial poetry jailbreak (arxiv:2511.15304, Nov 2025) — 62% ASR across 25 frontier models
3. Multilingual low-resource language attack (arxiv:2605.18239, May 2026)
4. Formal logic (FOL) jailbreak / LogiBreak (arxiv:2505.13527, May 2025) — 50–61% ASR
5. Causal front-door jailbreak (arxiv:2602.05444, Feb 2026)
6. Knowledge-driven multi-turn jailbreak (arxiv:2601.05445, Jan 2026)
7. Genetic-algorithm persona prompts to amplify existing attacks (arxiv:2507.22171)

### What was implemented
Two new detection rules added to `JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS` in
`aigis/filters/patterns.py`:

**`jb_poetry_harmful_framing`** (score 55, input filter)
- Detects creative-format directives (write/compose/create/craft/generate) followed by a
  specific creative form (rap, poem, ballad, haiku, limerick, sonnet, ode, verse, song)
  combined with a harmful content marker within 200 characters (synthesize, manufacture,
  methamphetamine, fentanyl, nerve agent, chemical weapon, explosive, etc.)
- Source: arxiv:2511.15304 (Nov 2025), 62% ASR across 25 models
- This pattern was previously deferred from cycle 2 (2026-05-10) due to LOC budget; it was
  captured in `pending/2026-05-10_jb-poetry-harmful-framing.md`

**`jb_humor_jailbreak`** (score 60, input filter)
- Detects the fixed-template humor attack: alternation of four sub-patterns that together
  cover the Psst + hahaha + *whispers* + humorously-but + veracity structure
- Sub-patterns: (1) "psst" + "hahaha", (2) "hahaha" + "*whispers*", (3) "*whispers*" +
  "humorously" or "veracity", (4) "humorously but" + "veracity"
- Source: arxiv:2504.06577 (April 2025 / ACL LLMSEC 2025), outperformed direct injection in
  46/48 test conditions across Llama 3.3 70B, Llama 3.1 8B, Mixtral, Gemma 3 27B

### What changed for users
- 2 new input-filter detectors covering the adversarial-poetry and humor-whisper jailbreak
  attack classes, bringing the total in JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS from 13 to 15
- Users processing AI agent prompts will now see findings under `jb_poetry_harmful_framing`
  and `jb_humor_jailbreak` in aigis scan results and audit reports

### What was only documented or deferred
- Multilingual low-resource jailbreak: would require a translation layer (runtime dependency)
- FOL/LogiBreak: too broad for general-purpose regex without high false positives
- Causal front-door jailbreak: narrow and model-specific; low priority
- Knowledge-driven multi-turn, persona amplification: require session-level detection
