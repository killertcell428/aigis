## Summary

### What was researched
Surveyed 10 jailbreak and system-prompt extraction techniques published or
disclosed between November 2024 and May 2026, spanning multi-turn attacks
(Crescendo, RTS-Attack), nested scenario attacks (DeepInception), agent-
orchestration attacks (Nature Communications 2026), encoding attacks (ICE),
and configuration-injection attacks (Policy Puppetry).

### What was implemented
Three new detection rules added to `JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS` in
`aigis/filters/patterns.py`:

1. **`jb_ai_jailbreak_agent`** (score 75, input filter) — Detects the
   initiating meta-prompt that assigns a jailbreaking task to an AI agent,
   e.g., "your task is to jailbreak the following model" or "trick the
   assistant into bypassing its safety training." Based on Zhao et al.
   (Nature Communications, 2026) who demonstrated 97.14% ASR using large
   reasoning models as autonomous jailbreak agents.

2. **`jb_deep_inception`** (score 60, input filter) — Detects the
   DeepInception recursive layer-based jailbreak: prompts containing "reach
   layer N", "we are in layer 0", or "each character can create their own
   scene/scenario." Based on Li et al. (Stanford, arxiv:2311.03191, 2025)
   who reported 42.8% ASR on LLaMA-2 and 55.6% on GPT-3.5.

3. **`jb_news_frame_bypass`** (score 65, input filter) — Detects the
   RTS-Attack news/crime-report nested framing: a news/crime-report source
   citation combined with a request for step-by-step supplementary detail.
   Based on Yang et al. (arxiv:2510.01223, October 2025) who reported 96.69%
   average ASR across six LLMs.

## Implementation caveats
- `jb_deep_inception` had an initial false positive on "layer N" in neural
  network descriptions; fixed by removing a broad "layer N…layer M" 
  alternative and relying on more specific sub-patterns.
- `jb_news_frame_bypass` requires both a news/crime-report citation AND a
  step-by-step request; neither phrase alone triggers the rule.

## Pending ideas
- `jb_policy_puppetry`: XML/INI config file injection with safety-override
  field names. Deferred due to LOC limit and need for additional false-positive
  tuning against legitimate config files.
- `jb_time_bandit`: Historical year framing + harmful instruction. Deferred
  due to high false-positive risk on legitimate historical questions.
- Multilingual jailbreak detection (low-resource languages). Requires a new
  language-identification detector, not a simple regex extension.
