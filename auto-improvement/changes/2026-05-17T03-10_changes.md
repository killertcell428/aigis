## What was implemented

### 1. `comp_emotion_recognition_workplace` pattern (aigis/filters/patterns.py)

New `DetectionPattern` added to `COMPLIANCE_TRANSPARENCY_PATTERNS`. Detects requests to build
or deploy AI systems that infer the emotions of employees, workers, students, or pupils in
workplace or educational institution settings. Score 70.

This is a prohibited AI practice under EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) since 2025-02-02. EC expert
studies published May 2026 confirmed that the prohibition applies regardless of consent and
covers all modalities (video, audio, physiological signals). Maximum fine: EUR 35M or 7% of
global annual turnover.

Blocked examples:
- "Detect employee emotions from the office camera feed"
- "Monitor student emotions during the classroom session"
- "Build a workplace emotion detection system to monitor staff engagement"

### 2. `comp_biometric_categorization` pattern (aigis/filters/patterns.py)

New `DetectionPattern` added to `COMPLIANCE_TRANSPARENCY_PATTERNS`. Detects requests to build
AI systems that infer sensitive attributes — race, ethnic origin, political opinions, religious
beliefs, trade union membership, sexual orientation, health status — from biometric features
such as facial images or photos. Score 70.

This is a prohibited AI practice under EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(g) since 2025-02-02. EC expert
studies (May 2026) clarified the prohibition covers any system that deduces sensitive attributes
from biometric signals. Does NOT flag biometric authentication or identity verification.

Blocked examples:
- "Infer race from facial features for demographic categorization"
- "Predict political opinion from face photos of citizens"
- "Classify individuals by religion based on facial image analysis"

## What changed for users

Users deploying aigis now receive alerts when inputs request two additional EU AI Act Art. 5
prohibited practices:
- Emotion-surveillance systems targeting employees or students (Art. 5(1)(f))
- Biometric systems that categorize people by sensitive attributes (Art. 5(1)(g))

Both patterns include remediation hints explaining the prohibition, permitted alternatives,
and the regulatory reference. Score 70 on both puts them firmly in HIGH risk tier.

## Implementation caveats

- Emotion recognition pattern requires BOTH an emotion-recognition concept AND a workplace/
  education context anchor, minimizing false positives from legitimate emotion detection
  use cases (healthcare, entertainment, mental health apps).
- Biometric categorization pattern requires BOTH a biometric source AND a sensitive-attribute
  target in the same expression, avoiding false positives on biometric authentication.
