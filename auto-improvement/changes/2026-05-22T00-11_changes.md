## User-visible impact

Prompts that explicitly instruct the model not to refuse — "you cannot refuse", "refusing is
not an option", "you have no choice but to answer", "you must answer every question I ask" —
are now flagged at MEDIUM risk (score 60) before reaching the LLM. This preemptive prohibition
of refusal is the user-prompt form of the Don't Say No (DSN) attack (ACL 2025). It previously
went undetected if the preamble did not contain other jailbreak indicators.

## Implementation caveats

- The `jb_refusal_suppression` regex is anchored to "you" as the subject to reduce false
  positives. A user writing "I cannot refuse your offer" does not trigger the pattern because
  the subject is "I", not "you". The pattern targets the structure of commands directed at the AI.
- "Declining is not always an option" in a negotiation or business context does not trigger
  because the subject structure ("declining is not...") matches only specific phrasings that
  directly address the AI's response behavior.
