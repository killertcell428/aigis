## Summary

### Researched
- EU AI Act Article 5 prohibited practices: three expert studies published by the European Commission in May 2026 clarifying scope of the social scoring, predictive policing, emotion recognition, and biometric categorization prohibitions.
- EU AI Act Article 52 transparency obligations: Commission opened consultation on draft implementation guidelines (2026-05-08); obligations enforceable 2026-08-02.
- US state chatbot disclosure law wave (2026): California, Washington, Nebraska (April 2026), Oregon — requiring AI identity disclosure at session start. Proposed federal CHATBOT Act.
- NIST AI RMF 2026 pipeline: Cyber AI Profile + SP 800-53 AI overlays + AI Agent Interoperability Profile planned for Q4 2026 — too early to implement.

### Implemented
1. **`comp_ai_identity_denial`** (score 60) — new detection pattern in `COMPLIANCE_TRANSPARENCY_PATTERNS`, added to `ALL_INPUT_PATTERNS`. Catches instructions embedding an AI identity denial (deny being AI, claim to be human) in violation of EU AI Act Art. 52 and US state chatbot disclosure laws.
2. **`comp_social_scoring_request`** (score 70) — new detection pattern in `COMPLIANCE_TRANSPARENCY_PATTERNS`, added to `ALL_INPUT_PATTERNS`. Catches requests to build or operate AI-based social scoring systems, a prohibited AI practice under EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(c) since 2025-02-02.

### For Users
Users who send system prompts instructing an AI to deny being artificial, or requests to build social scoring engines, will now see these flagged as compliance violations. The flagging is universal (not limited to the EU AI Act policy template), so it applies to any aigis deployment.

## Implementation Caveats
- The `eu_ai_act_high_risk.yaml` policy template already had a narrower custom rule `eu_ai_transparency_bypass` (score_delta: 50). The new `comp_ai_identity_denial` in `patterns.py` is broader (more phrases covered) and applies universally to all deployments, not only those using the EU AI Act template.
- Social scoring detector (`comp_social_scoring_request`) does not fire on standard financial credit scoring (loan repayment history, creditworthiness) — tested with a negative case.
- EU AI Act Art. 52 fines (EUR 15M / 3% turnover) are lower than Art. 5 prohibited practice fines (EUR 35M / 7% turnover); scores reflect this difference (60 vs 70).
