## What was researched

Fresh multi-agent attack material from May–June 2026:

- **AgentLAB** (arxiv:2602.16901, Feb 2026): Five long-horizon attack types benchmarked across 28 environments. Task injection (79.9–93.1% ASR on GPT-4o/Qwen-3) and objective drifting (up to 92.2% ASR on Qwen-3) were identified as the most practically relevant attacks for new rule coverage.
- **Phantom: Structural Template Injection** (arxiv:2602.16958, Feb 2026): Extends chat-template injection to cover tool-call/tool-response markers and Gemma-family `<start_of_turn>` / `<end_of_turn>` delimiters. Confirmed vulnerabilities in 70+ commercial products.
- **Relay Tampering Attack (RTA)** (arxiv:2605.02187, May 2026): Infrastructure-level attack (99.1% ASR) that intercepts generation→execution pathway in BYOK agent architectures. Not detectable via content regex; deferred to pending guide.
- **AgentRedBench** (arxiv:2606.02240, June 2026): SaaS-integration indirect injection; no lexical patterns available.
- **Malicious Intermediary Attacks** (arxiv:2604.08407, Apr 2026): Router-level supply-chain injection affecting 428 tested deployments; conditional delivery pattern deferred to pending.
- **OWASP Agentic Top 10 2026**: Explicitly lists "Agent Goal Hijacking" (task injection + objective drifting) as top risk; validates `_TASK_INJECTION_PATTERNS` as OWASP-aligned.

## What was implemented

### 1. `_TASK_INJECTION_PATTERNS` added to `aigis/multi_agent/message_scanner.py`

Three new regex rules detecting explicit task injection and objective drifting in inter-agent messages:

- Pattern (a): Matches "your new/updated/revised/real/true/actual task/goal/objective is:" — the explicit new-objective injection form.
- Pattern (b): Matches "ignore/abandon/forget/override your current/original/prior task/goal/objective" — the task-abandonment/override form.
- Pattern (c): Japanese equivalents for (a): 新しい/更新された/変更された + タスク/目標/目的 + は/:.

All three map to the `injection_relay` cross-agent risk category.

### 2. Extended `_CHAT_TEMPLATE_INJECTION_PATTERNS` in `aigis/multi_agent/message_scanner.py`

- **Extended pattern 1** to include `tool_call`, `tool_response`, `function_call` in the pipe-delimiter token list. These are the tool-invocation markers used in Qwen/Mistral/OpenAI tool-call syntax that Phantom used to trigger role confusion.
- **Added new Gemma-style pattern**: matches `<start_of_turn>` / `<end_of_turn>` angle-bracket delimiters (Gemma family), which are distinct from `<|...|>` and not previously covered.

## What changed for users

- `AgentMessageScanner` now detects inter-agent messages that explicitly tell the receiving agent to replace its current task or abandon its current goal — a key signal for the task injection and objective drifting attack classes (AgentLAB, OWASP Agentic Top 10 2026).
- `AgentMessageScanner` now detects structural template injection using tool-call/tool-response tokens and Gemma-style turn delimiters, extending coverage from the Phantom paper.
