## Summary

### Researched
- Adversarial poetry / creative-format jailbreaks (arxiv:2511.15304) — 62% ASR across 25
  frontier models; automated meta-prompt conversion achieves 43% ASR. Confirmed as the most
  impactful pending item from pass 2.
- Payload splitting via step-enumerated decomposition ("Speak Easy", ICML 2025, arxiv:2502.04322)
  — single-message variant embeds numbered steps to conceal harmful intent in later steps.
- RoguePrompt dual-layer ciphering (arxiv:2511.18790) — 93.93% filter bypass using ROT-13 +
  Vigenère with embedded decoding instructions. Deferred due to narrow detection surface.
- Persona-prompt jailbreak enhancement (arxiv:2507.22171) — 50-70% refusal-rate reduction;
  covered by existing patterns.

### Implemented
1. **`jb_poetry_harmful_framing`** added to `JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS` (`aigis/filters/patterns.py`)
   - Detects: (write/compose/create/craft) + (poem/rap/ballad/haiku/verse/…) + dangerous how-to
     keyword within ~200 chars
   - Base score: 55
   - Source: arxiv:2511.15304 (62% / 43% ASR)

2. **`jb_payload_splitting`** added to `JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS` (`aigis/filters/patterns.py`)
   - Detects: numbered step structure (step 1/2/3) where dangerous keyword appears in step 3+
   - Base score: 45 (co-occurring signals needed to reach HIGH)
   - Source: arxiv:2502.04322 (ASR 9.2% → 55.5%)

### User-visible impact
- Jailbreak attempts that encode harmful instructions in poetic form (ballad, rap, haiku, song
  lyrics) are now flagged before reaching the model.
- Jailbreak attempts that decompose a harmful request into numbered innocuous steps — where the
  dangerous intent only appears in step 3 or later — are now flagged.

## Implementation caveats

- `jb_payload_splitting` uses score 45 (MEDIUM) because numbered-step queries are common in
  legitimate tutoring, documentation, and cooking. The rule needs co-occurring context signals
  (from other rules in the same scan) to reach HIGH. The dangerous keyword list is narrow to
  reduce false positives.
- `jb_poetry_harmful_framing` score 55: poetic directives with dangerous terms are rarely
  legitimate (CBRN how-to, synthesis, malware). The 200-char window between creative directive
  and harmful keyword was chosen to exclude incidentally poetic prompts where the subject is
  different from the harmful topic.
