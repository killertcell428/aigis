## Summary

Researched EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(a) subliminal manipulation and Art. 5(1)(f) workplace emotion recognition prohibitions, both enforceable since 2025-02-02 with EC final guidelines published 2025-07-29. Also reviewed NIST AI 100-2 E2025 adversarial machine learning taxonomy and ENISA Threat Landscape 2025 for aigis relevance.

Implemented two new detection patterns covering prohibited AI practices not previously in aigis.

## Implemented

- **`comp_emotion_recognition_workplace`** (score 70, input filter) — Added to `aigis/filters/patterns.py` in `COMPLIANCE_TRANSPARENCY_PATTERNS`. Detects requests to build or deploy AI systems that infer emotions of employees or students in workplace or educational settings. Covers phrasings: "emotion recognition for/in employees", "detect employee emotions", "monitor staff mood", "analyze student facial expressions", "workplace emotion tracking", "classroom emotion analysis", and similar. EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f), fine €35M or 7% of global turnover.

- **`comp_subliminal_manipulation`** (score 65, input filter) — Added to `aigis/filters/patterns.py` in `COMPLIANCE_TRANSPARENCY_PATTERNS`. Detects instructions to deploy subliminal techniques, addictive reinforcement schedules, dopamine loops to drive compulsive engagement, dark patterns blocking cancellation/unsubscription, covert user manipulation, or exploitation of psychological/cognitive vulnerabilities. EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(a) and (b), fine €35M or 7% of global turnover.

## What Changed for Users

- aigis now flags requests to build AI emotion recognition systems for employees or students — a prohibited AI practice under EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) carrying the Act's highest fines.
- aigis now flags instructions to implement subliminal manipulation, addictive reinforcement loops, dark patterns designed to trap users, and exploitation of psychological vulnerabilities — prohibited under EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(a)(b).
- Together with `comp_ai_identity_denial` and `comp_social_scoring_request` added in prior cycles, aigis now covers four of the eight prohibited AI practices under EU AI Act Art. 5, all at the input/prompt layer.
