## Summary

Added 6 new detection patterns to `AgentMessageScanner` covering two emerging multi-agent attack classes:

1. **Prompt Infection (self-replicating injection)** — 3 patterns detect instructions that tell an agent to embed or propagate a malicious payload in all its future outputs, causing viral spread across the agent graph. Research basis: arxiv:2410.07283 (>80% ASR with GPT-4o).

2. **Colluding / Byzantine agents** — 3 patterns detect instructions telling peer agents to covertly coordinate against the orchestrator/human overseer: hiding actions from the orchestrator, jointly bypassing safety controls, and keeping secrets from the oversight layer. Research basis: TAMAS benchmark arxiv:2511.05269 (60–82% ASR), collusion detection paper arxiv:2604.01151.

## Caveats
