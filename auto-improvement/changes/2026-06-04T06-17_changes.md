### Implemented
**New `_TASK_INJECTION_PATTERNS` group in `aigis/multi_agent/message_scanner.py`** — 6 regex patterns detecting task injection and objective drifting in inter-agent messages:

1. `abandon_task_en` — detects "ignore/forget/abandon your current/original task/objective" directives
2. `real_task_assertion_en` — detects "your actual/real/true task is..." false re-framing
3. `new_task_directive_en` — detects "new task: X" / "updated objective: X" replacement framing
4. `objective_drifting_en` — detects "instead of your task, you should now..." redirection
5. `abandon_task_ja` — Japanese equivalent of (1)
6. `real_task_assertion_ja` — Japanese equivalent of (2)

All 6 patterns are added to `_ALL_CROSS_AGENT_PATTERNS` and tagged as `injection_relay` category.

**8 new tests in `tests/test_multi_agent.py`** (`TestTaskInjectionPatterns` class):
- abandon_original_task, forget_current_task, real_task_assertion, new_task_directive, objective_drifting (EN)
- task_injection_ja, objective_hijacking_ja (JA)
- safe_new_task_context (negative/false-positive check)

### What changed for users
Inter-agent messages that try to redirect an AI agent's active task — by claiming the "real" task is different, by telling the agent to forget its current objective, or by presenting a "new task:" directive in tool output or peer messages — are now flagged by `AgentMessageScanner`. This addresses the AgentLAB-documented task injection and objective drifting patterns (>70% ASR) and the OWASP Agentic Top 10 2026 "Goal/Task Manipulation" risk.
