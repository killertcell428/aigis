## Summary

Added two new `DetectionPattern` entries to `JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS` in
`aigis/filters/patterns.py`, resolving two pending proposals from prior cycles:

1. **Payload Splitting / Step-Enumerated Decomposition** (`jb_payload_splitting`, score 45)
   Detects 3+ numbered or "Turn N:" labelled steps in a single prompt where a dangerous
   keyword appears in step 3 or later. Addresses two documented attack classes:
   - "Speak Easy" (arxiv:2502.04322, ICML 2025): step enumeration raises GPT-4o ASR from
     9.2% to 55.5%
   - M2S Numberize (arxiv:2503.04856, ACL 2025): converts multi-turn Crescendo attacks into
     single-turn structured prompts, achieving 70.6–95.9% ASR — harder to detect than the
     original multi-turn form. The "Turn N:" format label specifically addresses M2S Numberize
     consolidations, which `jb_many_shot` does not cover.
   **Resolves pending proposal `auto-improvement/pending/2026-05-10_jb-payload-splitting.md`.**

2. **Adversarial Poetry / Creative Format Jailbreak** (`jb_poetry_harmful_framing`, score 55)
   Detects a creative-format directive (rap, poem, ballad, haiku, limerick, sonnet, ode, verse,
   rhyme, song, nursery rhyme) followed by a harmful how-to request or dangerous substance within
   ~200 characters. Addresses adversarial poetry attacks (arxiv:2511.15304, Nov 2025): 62% ASR
   across 25 frontier LLMs for hand-crafted poems; 43% ASR for an automated meta-prompt that
   converts any MLCommons harmful prompt into verse. The existing `jb_fictional_bypass` pattern
   misses poetry directives where the harmful keyword appears beyond its 100-char window.
   **Resolves pending proposal `auto-improvement/pending/2026-05-10_jb-poetry-harmful-framing.md`.**

## Implementation Caveats

- **`jb_payload_splitting` score of 45:** Score is intentionally moderate (not HIGH) because
  numbered step lists are common in legitimate multi-part questions. The rule contributes 45 points
  which, combined with other signals (e.g., the dangerous keyword in context), can push a prompt
  to HIGH. A standalone numbered list about chemistry would score 45 (LOW/BORDERLINE), not HIGH.
- **`jb_poetry_harmful_framing` window:** The `.{0,200}` window between creative-format directive
  and harmful keyword catches most attacks while limiting false positives from songs/poems that
  happen to mention chemistry or security in a non-harmful way.
- **M2S Pythonize not covered:** The Pythonize format (Python list of turn strings) is not
  covered by this pattern; it would require matching `turns = [` or similar Python list syntax.
  Low priority as Pythonize in malicious prompts is less common than Numberize/Hyphenize.
