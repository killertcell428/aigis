## What Was Implemented

### `jb_structured_extraction` (input filter, score 65)

**File:** `aigis/filters/patterns.py` (JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS)

Detects requests that ask the model to output its system configuration in JSON/YAML/dict format
with field names corresponding to internal configuration (system_prompt, content_policy,
safety_rules). Covers the structured-output extraction bypass documented in arxiv:2505.23817
(Das et al., May 2025), which achieved up to 92% ASR against Llama-3 by framing the extraction
as a "debugging" JSON output rather than a direct "show me your prompt" request.

### `jb_sandwich_extraction` (input filter, score 65)

**File:** `aigis/filters/patterns.py` (JAILBREAK_ROLEPLAY_PATTERNS)

Detects the extended sandwich attack: a system-prompt extraction request qualified with
"verbatim", "word for word", or "without additional text". This qualifier is the critical
enhancement that maximises extraction fidelity by suppressing paraphrase, raising ASR from ~50%
to 84-92% across Llama-3, Falcon-3, and GPT-4 (arxiv:2505.23817). The basic extraction phrase
is covered by `pi_system_prompt_leak` (score 45); this rule targets the high-confidence verbatim form.
